# Product defaults for open questions

CLAUDE.md and AGENT.md leave these questions open. Until the product owner decides otherwise, the
app uses the conservative defaults below and **discloses them in the UI and reports**. Changing a
default later needs a migration or a schedule revision, so decide early where possible.

| #   | Question                 | Default used                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Status                              |
| --- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1   | Device transfer method   | File-based full-workspace JSON export/import only. No direct device-to-device transfer, LAN, or sync.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Default                             |
| 2   | Foreign currency         | GHS only. Other currencies cannot be entered. Currency is still stored on every amount so FX can be added later.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Default                             |
| 3   | Recognition basis        | **Service-date / incurred basis.** Revenue counts in the period of its service date. Expenses count in the period of their expense date. Payment status may be recorded but does not drive recognition. Reports label this as "management basis, not cash receipts".                                                                                                                                                                                    | Default — confirm                   |
| 4a  | Depreciation first month | **Full month from the in-service month.** The month containing the in-service date receives a full monthly charge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Default — confirm                   |
| 4b  | Depreciation last month  | The final scheduled month absorbs rounding so the total equals the depreciable basis exactly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Default                             |
| 4c  | Disposal policy          | **Not implemented in MVP.** Assets can be archived. Retire/dispose with gain/loss is blocked until a policy is approved, as AGENT.md §6 requires.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Needs decision before disposal work |
| 4d  | Closed periods           | **Explicit period close.** The user closes depreciation months through a chosen ended month (audited, irreversible). Before any close, corrections regenerate the whole schedule as a new version. After close, closed lines are preserved, only residual value and useful life may change, and the remaining book value is spread over the remaining life from the first open month. Cost and in-service date are locked once their months are closed. | Default — confirm                   |
| 4e  | Archiving                | Archiving only hides an asset from the default list. Depreciation continues and history is kept. It is not a disposal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Default                             |
| 4f  | Useful life on revision  | Useful life is always the **total** life from the in-service month, including months already charged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Default                             |
| 3a  | Corrections and voids    | **Correct or void, never delete.** Revenue and expenses can be corrected while not voided; every changed field is audited. Voiding needs a reason (≥ 3 characters), is permanent, keeps the record visible, and removes it from every total. There is no period lock for revenue/expenses yet (only depreciation has period close).                                                                                                                     | Default — confirm                   |
| 3b  | Dates                    | Revenue and expense dates cannot be after today in the business time zone. Bookings for future trips are not recorded (no dispatch in MVP).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Default                             |
| 3c  | Unassigned records       | Revenue may be saved without an asset, and expenses as "unassigned", only by an explicit choice. They count in fleet totals but in no asset's results and are listed as needing attention until assigned.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Default                             |
| 3d  | Capital items            | An expense marked capital (acquisition or improvement) is recorded but never counted as an operating expense. It does not change depreciation automatically; increasing an asset's depreciable cost is a separate depreciation revision.                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Default — confirm                   |
| 3e  | Shared expenses          | Shared/fleet expenses are never allocated to assets. They are shown separately and will be deducted only at fleet level.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Confirmed (CLAUDE.md)               |
| 5   | Task categories          | Only data-backed tasks: incomplete asset setup, unassigned/review items, schedule changes awaiting confirmation. Registration/insurance/inspection reminders only if those dates are captured on the asset. No maintenance tasks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Default — confirm                   |
| —   | Week start               | Monday (configurable in settings)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Default                             |
| —   | Business timezone        | `Africa/Accra` (UTC+0, no daylight saving; configurable)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Default                             |
| —   | Rounding                 | Half away from zero to the pesewa; see ADR 0002                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Default                             |
| —   | Period boundaries        | Inclusive start date, inclusive end date, by business-timezone calendar date                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Default                             |
